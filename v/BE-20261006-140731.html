How it is computed

No usable earnings yet, so this values revenue. Value in five years = revenue in year five × an exit price-to-sales multiple, divided by the growth in the share count. The exit multiple is a mature net margin times the market's P/E: in five years the company is valued like the average company.

Revenue after the two consensus years fades toward 8% a year, keeping 30% of each year's excess — the persistence measured for the 1,000 largest companies over 1950-2015 (Mauboussin et al., The Base Rate Book). The starting rate is capped at 122%, the 98th percentile of one-year sales growth (Chan, Karceski & Lakonishok 2003); the bull case lifts the cap.
