How it is computed

Analysts expect its earnings to shrink, so this values revenue. Value in five years = today's price × the growth in revenue ÷ the growth in the share count. My valuation holds today's price for each $1 of sales, so the price grows exactly as fast as revenue per share — the earnings model's logic, on sales.
