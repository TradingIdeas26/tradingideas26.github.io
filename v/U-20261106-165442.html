How it is computed

Analysts publish no five-year earnings growth for it, so this values revenue. Value in five years = today's price × the growth in revenue ÷ the growth in the share count. My valuation holds today's price for each $1 of sales, so the price grows exactly as fast as revenue per share — the earnings model's logic, on sales.

Revenue after the two consensus years fades toward 8% a year, keeping 30% of each year's excess — the persistence measured for the 1,000 largest companies over 1950-2015 (Mauboussin et al., The Base Rate Book). The starting rate is capped at 122%, the 98th percentile of one-year sales growth (Chan, Karceski & Lakonishok 2003).
