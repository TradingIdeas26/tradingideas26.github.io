How it is computed

Price in five years = next-year earnings × (1 + growth)5 × the P/E in five years. My valuation takes the analysts' consensus growth and holds today's P/E, so the price grows exactly as fast as earnings.

The stress test corrects two things. Five-year growth forecasts run hot: in Chan, Karceski & Lakonishok (2003) the most optimistic fifth of companies was forecast to grow 22.4% a year and delivered 9.5%, so growth here is 1% + 0.42 × the forecast. And a multiple above the market's is assumed to drift back toward it; one below it is not assumed to rise.
